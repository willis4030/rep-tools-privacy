Rep Tools Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Rep Tools uses Expo/EAS Observe to collect limited analytics about app usage, performance, and product searches. These analytics help improve the app and guide future development.

Analytics may include SKU searches, random installation and session identifiers, device details, and app version. They help us troubleshoot problems. Our analytics are designed to exclude personal details and user content.

Expo/EAS processes and stores analytics as described in the Expo Privacy Policy. EAS Observe metric data is retained for at least 60 days.

GitHub hosts this page and may collect technical information about visits under its Privacy Statement.
