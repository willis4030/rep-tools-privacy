Rep Tools Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This policy explains how Rep Tools uses app analytics and handles information you provide when contacting us.

App usage and search analytics

Rep Tools uses Expo Application Services (EAS) Observe to understand app usage, measure performance, and troubleshoot problems. Analytics may include:

  • A pseudonymous identifier for an app installation and identifiers for individual app sessions.
  • Device model, operating system, app version and build, event timestamps, and technical performance or diagnostic information.
  • Feature usage and SKU search activity, including the product SKU codes searched and search outcomes, such as whether results were found.

These identifiers let us group activity across sessions without requiring your name or email address. Pseudonymous analytics can still distinguish one installation from another; they are not completely anonymous.

Information excluded from analytics

We do not intentionally collect surgeon names, hospital names, patient information, free-text notes or other free-text content, or the contents of uploaded files in analytics. SKU analytics are intended to record product codes rather than unrestricted search text. Please avoid entering personal or sensitive information into SKU searches.

Why we use this information

We use analytics to improve Rep Tools, understand which tools and product lookups are useful, identify missing or unsuccessful SKU searches, and investigate errors and performance issues.

Third-party processing and retention

Expo/EAS processes and stores analytics for Rep Tools. Its handling of information is described in the Expo Privacy Policy. Expo currently documents a minimum retention period of 60 days for EAS Observe metric data; actual retention may be longer and varies with the service and data type.

This policy page is hosted by GitHub Pages. When you visit it, GitHub may process technical request information, including your IP address, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. This page contains no app analytics scripts, forms, or cookies set by Rep Tools.

Please do not send patient information or sensitive file contents.
